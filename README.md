# Forever Gear Lab

A fan-made tool for the WoW Forever server: it reads your Auctionator scan and your character,
simulates fights in the browser, and tells you which auction house items are worth buying.

Live at https://gearlab.zohadigital.co

Your files are read in your browser and never uploaded. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
Class and spell numbers come from the wowsims/forever project's data; item stats from Wowhead's WoW Forever tooltips.
