Where is the folder?
  1. In the Battle.net app, open the gear menu next to Play and choose Show in Explorer. That opens the World of Warcraft folder.
  2. Pick that folder here. The site finds the rest on its own.
  3. If the browser refuses the folder (it does for some protected locations such as Program Files), use "Choose the files myself" and go to:
World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Account\YOUR-ACCOUNT\SavedVariables
